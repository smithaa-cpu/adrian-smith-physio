Specialties

Where I focus my practice

A closer look at the five areas patients most often come to me for.

Golf

Golf Physiotherapy

I'm TPI (Titleist Performance Institute) Level 1 certified, and offer dedicated TPI assessments and physio sessions built specifically around the demands of the golf swing. Whether you're dealing with pain that's affecting your game or looking to improve your mobility, rotation, and swing mechanics, sessions start with identifying the physical limitations holding your swing back, then build a plan to address them — whether you're a weekend golfer or play competitively.

Athletic Performance

General Athletic Population & Strength/Conditioning

I work with active people who push their bodies hard — hikers, runners, rock climbers, soccer players, and everyone in between. That includes rehab for a wide range of sport and activity-related injuries, including ACL repairs, with an evidence-based approach focused on building real strength and capacity, not just treating pain — so you can get back to the activities you care about with confidence.

Older Adult

Older Adult & Bone/Joint Health

Through McMaster University's PACE seniors program, I support older adults in building and maintaining strength, balance, and joint health so they can stay active and independent. Sessions are tailored to individual mobility levels and goals, whether that's fall-prevention, managing arthritis, or simply staying strong for everyday life.

Cardiac

Cardiac Experience

Through McMaster PACE's cardiac rehabilitation program, I work with patients recovering from cardiac events — including heart attacks and cardiac surgery — helping them safely and gradually rebuild activity tolerance and confidence in a structured, supported setting.

Post-Surgical

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

I help patients recover from a range of orthopedic surgeries, including total hip and knee replacements, shoulder rotator cuff repairs, and fractures or broken bones. Treatment is built around each stage of healing — from early mobility and protecting the surgical site, through to rebuilding strength and returning safely to daily life or activity.
